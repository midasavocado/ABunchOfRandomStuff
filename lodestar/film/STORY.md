# LODESTAR — the film

*A short film in seven movements, scored and rendered entirely from code.*

## Logline

A small ship called the **Wren** leaves home carrying one keepsake, an old pocket watch.
A storm tears time apart and leaves the ship dead in the dark. Then a single star appears,
and the Wren follows it home.

## The two heroes

- **The Wren.** A small white ceramic ship with swept wings, twin blue engines and a thin
  line of cockpit light. It's never shown with a pilot. The ship is the character.
- **The Watch.** A skeleton pocket watch, with its brass gears and ruby jewels visible through
  the dial. It is the film's heartbeat: the second hand moves on every tick of the score's
  clock. When the music speeds time up, the watch races. When the ship is lost, the watch
  cracks. When the ship is home, the watch finally stops.

## Visual rules

- 2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen, with letterbox bars on a 16:9 frame.
- **Home** is warm amber and dawn gold. **The Storm** is crimson, magenta and bruise-purple.
  **The Lodestar** is pure blue-white light. **Lost** is almost black.
- Real motion blur, depth of field, bloom, anamorphic flares, film grain and a filmic grade.
- Cuts land on the music: engine ignition on the timpani, lightning on the brass hits,
  and a hard cut to black on the breath before the climax.
