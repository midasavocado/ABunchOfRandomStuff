# Spare Parts: main title theme

This is the opening theme for an imaginary sitcom about four inventors who share a garage. It is a 40-second, fast-talking recap of the whole history of invention.

- `index.html` plays the song and the animated blueprint opening in your browser. The band is synthesized live with the Web Audio API. The sung vocal is embedded in the page.
- `spare-parts-opening.mp4` is the same opening rendered to video (1280×720, 30 fps, AAC audio).
- `tools/` makes the vocal (a neural text-to-speech voice made to sing, then re-timed and pitch-corrected) and tests pitch, timing, words, and sync. See `tools/README.md` for the method and results.

G major · 160 BPM · 4/4 · 27 bars

## Lyrics

> In the beginning somebody whacked two rocks together, CLACK!
> Out popped a spark, it caught a twig, and mammoth steaks for snacks!
> Someone rolled a log downhill and hollered, “Hey, a wheel!”
> Levers, pulleys, watermills, and swords of shining steel!
>
> Clockwork gears went tick-a-tock, the printing press went round,
> The steam train huffed and puffed along with a ca-choo-choo sound!
> Lightbulbs, wires, telephones, the radio’s first hum,
> Transistors, chips, and rocket ships, look how far we’ve come!
>
> Every bolt and blueprint that the ages left behind
> Is piled up in a garage with four brilliant, broken minds!
> It all fits together… *(sort of!)*
> **SPARE PARTS!**
